Interstellar Horizons (2026) – Review

Directed by Alex Garland • Sci-Fi / Drama • 8.8 / 10

A visually stunning exploration of human persistence, deep space travel, and time manipulation. The film balances hard science fiction concepts with raw, emotional depth that resonates across every scene.

The cinematic scale is complemented by a powerful, pulsating sound design. Every silent passage across the cosmic void serves as an emotional crescendo. For enthusiasts of cerebral world-building, this entry marks a defining achievement in modern cinema storytelling.
