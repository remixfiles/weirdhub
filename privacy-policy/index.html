Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Welcome to WeirdHub (accessible at weirdhub.site). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following instances:

  • Email Subscriptions: When you subscribe to our weekly newsletter, we collect your email address solely to deliver our curated digest of stories.
  • Contact Form Submissions: If you reach out through our contact page, we collect your name, email address, and message content to address your inquiry.
  • Log & Usage Data: Like most websites, our servers automatically collect non-personally identifiable information such as browser type, referring URLs, operating system, and timestamp data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data strictly for legitimate operational purposes, including:

  • Delivering the weekly WeirdHub newsletter to subscribed readers.
  • Responding to user messages, suggestions, and support requests.
  • Monitoring website performance, security, and user experience.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal email address or contact details to third parties.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Cookies

WeirdHub partners with third-party advertising networks to support our platform. These advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to serve relevant ads based on your visits to this and other websites across the internet.

You can manage or disable cookies at any time through your individual web browser settings.

4. Email Unsubscribe & Data Rights

You have full control over your personal data. If you no longer wish to receive our weekly stories, you can request removal from our subscription list at any time. You may also request the deletion of any contact messages you previously sent to us by reaching out through our Contact Us page.

5. Security of Your Information

We implement appropriate technical measures to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of user data. However, please be aware that no electronic transmission over the internet is completely immune to risks.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect site improvements or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted here with an updated revision date.

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this policy, please reach out via our Contact Page.
