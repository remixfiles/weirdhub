Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to WeirdHub (accessible at weirdhub.site). WeirdHub is an independent, research-oriented digital magazine and content platform dedicated to curating the world’s most eccentric, extraordinary, and verified anomalies from legitimate historical, scientific, and media sources. We operate as an open-access reading environment that does not require user accounts, passwords, or registration to browse our publications.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy details our operational practices concerning the collection, usage, safeguarding, and disclosure of personal and non-personal data when you visit our website, subscribe to our newsletter, interact with our contact desks, or encounter third-party services and advertisements on our domain.

Consent & Applicability
By accessing, browsing, or utilizing WeirdHub, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to the practices outlined in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue the use of our website. Where required by applicable data protection laws, specific consent may be requested separately.

1. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

WeirdHub is designed with a privacy-first, lightweight architecture:

  • No Direct Database Tracking: WeirdHub does not independently harvest, store, or profile your personal IP addresses, device identifiers, browser specifications, operating systems, or geographic coordinates.
  • No Account System: We do not feature user registration, logins, or public comment forums; you can read our entire archive anonymously.
  • No Direct Tracking Cookies: WeirdHub does not set proprietary first-party tracking cookies to monitor individual visitor identities across web sessions.

2. Information You Provide Voluntarily

We receive and process personal information solely when you provide it to us voluntarily via the following channels:

  • Contact Form & Direct Inquiries: When reaching out via our Contact Us page or emailing our editorial desk directly, you voluntarily submit your Name, Email Address, Subject, and Message content. These details are delivered directly to our designated Gmail infrastructure and are used exclusively to address your inquiry, review editorial corrections, or provide assistance.
  • Free Weekly Newsletter Subscription: When opting into our free weekly newsletter digest, you provide your Name and Email Address. This data is utilized solely to deliver our curated weekly editorial stories and administer your subscription.
  • No Commercial Data Sale: We strictly never sell, monetize, rent, or lease your email address or personal details to third-party data brokers or marketing agencies. Unapproved disclosure of subscriber records is strictly prohibited.

3. Third-Party Infrastructure, Analytics & Advertising

While WeirdHub does not independently track visitors, we utilize industry-standard third-party service providers to host, secure, measure, and monetize our publication. These external platforms may automatically process technical, operational, and usage data according to their independent privacy standards:

  • Hosting & Security (GitHub Pages & Cloudflare): WeirdHub is hosted via GitHub Pages and routed through Cloudflare's Content Delivery Network (CDN). Cloudflare and GitHub may automatically log technical connection metrics, routing data, and system logs to protect against DDoS attacks, enforce operational security, and ensure fast global delivery.
  • Website Analytics (Google Analytics): We utilize Google Analytics to understand overall audience patterns, measure article popularity, and improve user experience. Google Analytics collects anonymized interaction metrics—such as page dwell time, referral sources, and device categories—under Google’s independent privacy practices. WeirdHub does not maintain personal databases derived from these analytics.
  • Advertising Networks (Adsterra & Future Partners): To sustain our publication, WeirdHub currently displays advertisements served by the Adsterra network. Adsterra may employ third-party cookies, tracking pixels, or device identifiers to serve, calibrate, and evaluate the performance of contextual or interest-based ads. Additionally, WeirdHub may integrate Google AdSense or other reputable advertising networks in the future, which will similarly handle data in accordance with their respective compliance policies.
  • Communication Channels (Google Workspace / Gmail): Contact submissions and direct reader inquiries are processed securely through Google’s Gmail mail infrastructure.

4. Cookies and Web Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small data files placed on your browser by websites you visit. While WeirdHub does not set first-party tracking cookies, our third-party partners (such as Google Analytics and Adsterra) may place cookies or similar tracking technologies on your device to execute analytics and display advertisements.

Managing Cookies: You have full control over cookie permissions. You can configure your web browser settings to reject all cookies, notify you when a cookie is placed, or delete existing cookies entirely. Please note that completely disabling cookies may alter display parameters or influence the relevance of advertisements served on our platform.

5. How We Use Collected Information

Any information accessed or received by WeirdHub is used strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To review, respond to, and resolve user inquiries, editorial suggestions, and factual correction notices.
  • To distribute our free weekly newsletter to confirmed subscribers.
  • To evaluate aggregated website traffic patterns through Google Analytics to understand content performance and improve platform navigation.
  • To maintain site stability, identify malicious activity, and protect our infrastructure through third-party security shields.

6. External Links & Source References

WeirdHub is built upon documented research and verified journalism. Consequently, our articles feature external links to peer-reviewed journals, historical archives, global news outlets, and reference databases. We also maintain social presence links to Facebook and Twitter/X.

Please note that clicking any outbound link directs you to an external domain operating outside the authority and control of WeirdHub. We do not govern and are not responsible for the privacy policies, data collection procedures, or content of third-party platforms. We strongly advise reviewing the individual privacy policies of any external destination you visit.

7. Data Retention & Information Security

We take appropriate security measures to safeguard user interactions:

  • Data Retention: Voluntary contact inquiries submitted to our Gmail inbox are retained only as long as necessary to address the matter, after which they are archived or deleted. Subscription records are maintained until a user opts out. Server logs managed by third-party services (such as Cloudflare or GitHub) follow their respective internal data retention policies.
  • Security Protocols: WeirdHub enforces end-to-end SSL/HTTPS encryption, ensuring that all data exchanged between your browser and our website is securely encrypted in transit.
  • Internet Security Disclaimer: While we rely on modern security infrastructure provided by Cloudflare and GitHub, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely infallible. We strive to protect your data using reasonable measures, but absolute, risk-free security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

WeirdHub is an informational, general-audience reading platform and is not designed for or intentionally directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 within the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from minors.

If a parent or guardian discovers that a child has submitted personal details through our contact or newsletter form without parental consent, please contact us immediately. Upon verification, we will promptly delete the specified information from our active records.

9. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA Principles)

Regardless of your geographic location, WeirdHub respects your fundamental data rights:

  • Access, Rectification & Erasure: You have the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of any personal information (such as contact messages or newsletter subscription details) held within our direct systems.
  • Unsubscribe at Any Time: Newsletter subscribers can opt out at any point by contacting us or following the unsubscribe directions provided within our communications. Upon unsubscribing, your address will be promptly removed from active dispatches.
  • Submitting a Request: To exercise any data rights, submit your request through our Contact Us page. We will process legitimate, verifiable requests within a reasonable timeframe, subject to any legal or security retention obligations.

10. International Data Transfers

WeirdHub operates globally through cloud-based infrastructure. The third-party services we utilize—including GitHub Pages, Cloudflare, Google Analytics, Adsterra, and Google Workspace (Gmail)—maintain distributed servers and data centers internationally. As a result, non-personal diagnostic data or voluntary communications may be transmitted, processed, and stored on servers located outside your country of residence, under the regulatory standards and data protection frameworks of those respective jurisdictions.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

WeirdHub reserves the right to revise, modify, or update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect site enhancements, technical changes, or evolving regulatory standards. Any modifications will be published directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top. Your continued interaction with WeirdHub following any revisions constitutes your acceptance of the updated terms.
