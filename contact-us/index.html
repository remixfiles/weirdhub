Submission Guidelines

  • General Feedback & Inquiries: There is no rigid format for general questions or feedback. Simply describe your thoughts or questions clearly.
  • Factual Corrections Policy: To maintain archival integrity, valid source citations are mandatory for factual corrections. Submissions based merely on speculation without credible references will not be considered.
  • Copyright & Content Removal: Copyright claims must include the specific article link and valid documentation proving ownership. Upon receipt of valid proof, our editorial desk will promptly modify or remove the contested material.
  • Advertising & Partnerships: For sponsorship or ad inquiries, including brand name, campaign format, proposed budget, timeline, and target audience helps expedite discussions. Further partnership details will be communicated via email.
  • Technical & Security Reports: If reporting 404 errors, broken links, image loading glitches, spam, or security vulnerabilities, please include the specific page URL, device/browser details, and screenshots if possible.
