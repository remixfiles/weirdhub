A Mother Wanted to Teach Her 17-Year-Old Son How Hard Life Without School Is, Then He Made $1,400 in Ten Days

By WeirdHub Team • 13 November, 2026

The Lesson That Backfired

In September 2024, in the eastern Chinese city of Jiaxing in Zhejiang province, a mother named Deng faced a dilemma familiar to parents around the world. Her 17-year-old son, Shen, was enrolled in a culinary vocational school, yet his academic performance had begun to decline steadily. When Deng confronted him about his slipping grades, the teenager told her plainly that studying felt pointless to him. Rather than lecturing him endlessly at the kitchen table, Deng decided to show him firsthand what life looks like when a person relies entirely on physical labor instead of formal education. Her intention was straightforward and rooted in parental concern. She wanted her son to experience the exhaustion, long hours, and daily pressure of working on the streets so that he would appreciate the relative comfort of sitting in a classroom. Following her suggestion, Shen modified an electric bike into a mobile food stall selling fried chicken and breakfast snacks. Deng fully expected the grueling reality of street vending to overwhelm the teenager within a few days, convincing him to abandon the streets and return willingly to his books and culinary classes. Instead, the plan triggered an outcome neither of them could have predicted. Rather than shrinking away from the physical demands of running a food stall, Shen adapted to the work with remarkable speed and enthusiasm, turning what was meant to be a harsh reality check into the launching pad for his own independent livelihood.

Ten Days and Ten Thousand Yuan

Once Shen rolled his modified electric bike out onto the streets of Jiaxing, he quickly discovered that he had a natural instinct for the fast-paced rhythm of street food vending. Preparing food, managing orders, serving hungry customers, and handling daily sales came easily to him, and his vocational background in cooking suddenly found an immediate, practical purpose outside the classroom. Within just ten days of launching his mobile stall, the 17-year-old earned 10,000 yuan, a sum equivalent to roughly 1,400 US dollars. For a teenager who had previously felt disconnected from his studies, earning that amount of money through his own physical effort in less than two weeks was nothing short of transformative. The financial reward proved to him that hard work could yield tangible, immediate results, completely undermining the lesson his mother had hoped to impart. Rather than feeling discouraged by the strain of manual labor, Shen fell in love with the independence and sense of accomplishment that came with running his own small enterprise. He soon made the bold decision to drop out of school altogether so that he could dedicate his full attention to his growing street food business. To sustain his momentum, Shen adopted a demanding daily routine that required immense discipline. Every morning, he began preparing his ingredients and food at nine o'clock, ensuring everything was ready before heading out to his vending spot at four in the afternoon. Reaching his location required him to ride his electric bike roughly 13 kilometers each way, and he continued serving customers late into the night, not returning home until three o'clock in the morning.

Respecting an Unconventional Path

Watching her son trade his formal education for a punishing schedule of late-night street vending left Deng torn between traditional expectations and the undeniable work ethic unfolding right in front of her. Initially, she tried to persuade Shen to reconsider his decision and return to school, holding onto the hope that he might still complete his formal studies. However, as she watched him wake up willingly each day to prepare food, travel 13 kilometers on his electric bike, and work tirelessly until three in the morning without complaint, her perspective began to shift. Shen was not drifting aimlessly or avoiding responsibility; on the contrary, he was demonstrating a level of self-reliance, focus, and determination that few teenagers his age ever display. He even began planning the next phase of his business, setting a clear goal to save enough money from his stall to purchase an electric tricycle that would allow him to expand his operations. Recognizing that her efforts to push him back into the classroom were not going to change his mind, Deng ultimately chose to accept and respect her son's path. She acknowledged the genuine pride she felt in watching him work hard and stand on his own two feet, noting that as long as her son remained healthy, happy, and stayed away from anything illegal, she was content with the choice he had made. What started as a mother's attempt to prove the hardship of life without school ended up revealing a young entrepreneur who thrived on the very challenges meant to deter him.
