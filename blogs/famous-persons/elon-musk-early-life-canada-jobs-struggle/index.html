From Shoveling Grain to Cleaning Hazardous Boilers: The Untold Story of Elon Musk's Early Struggles in Canada

By WeirdHub Team • 8 November, 2026

A Courageous Journey Across the Globe with Limited Savings

Long before he became a household name and one of the world's wealthiest entrepreneurs, Elon Musk experienced a humble and arduous beginning far removed from the tech empires he would later build. In June 1989, at the age of seventeen, Musk made the pivotal decision to leave South Africa and relocate to Canada, turning eighteen shortly after his arrival. Leaving his home country behind, he arrived in Montreal equipped with little more than a single backpack and a heavy suitcase filled entirely with books. Financially, his departure was made possible through contributions from his parents, Errol Musk and Maye Musk, who together provided him with approximately four thousand dollars. However, after purchasing his plane ticket to cross the Atlantic, Musk was left with roughly two thousand five hundred dollars to build a new life in North America. Upon landing in Montreal, his initial plan was to move in with a relative residing in the city. To his surprise, he discovered upon arrival that the relative had already moved elsewhere, leaving him stranded without a permanent place to stay. Facing immediate uncertainty, the young traveler checked into a local hostel to figure out his next steps. Demonstrating resourcefulness, he purchased a pass for the Greyhound bus network and embarked on an extensive journey across the Canadian landscape toward the western provinces. His journey eventually brought him to the small area of Waldeck in Saskatchewan, where he found shelter and work on a farm owned by a distant relative named Mark Tulon. On this remote farm, Musk adapted to demanding physical labor. He spent long hours working in wheat and vegetable fields, using a shovel to clear out grain storage bins, caring for crops, and assisting in the construction of farm barns. This initial period in rural Canada marked the start of a series of physically demanding jobs that defined his formative years.

Braving Extreme Hazards in the Boilers of British Columbia

Seeking new opportunities after his time on the farm, Elon Musk eventually moved further west to the province of British Columbia. In this new location, he took on work at a lumber mill, spending his days using saws to cut timber. Wanting to earn as much money as possible to support himself, Musk visited a local employment assistance center to search for the highest-paying job available. The center pointed him toward an exceptionally difficult position that offered an attractive rate of eighteen dollars an hour, which was significantly higher than standard manual labor wages at the time. The role required cleaning out the interior of a toxic boiler room at a wood processing factory. As documented in Walter Isaacson's biography of Musk as well as Musk's own personal recollections, this job proved to be the most grueling and hazardous work experience of his life. To perform the task, workers had to wear full protective clothing to guard against intense heat and dangerous materials. Musk had to crawl through extremely narrow, stifling, and unventilated tunnels to reach the interior of the heated boiler. Once inside the dark and cramped space, he had to use a shovel to clear out toxic sand, accumulated ash, and heavy industrial waste. The environment was suffocating and physically draining, requiring intense stamina and tolerance for extreme conditions. The work carried severe risks, as remaining inside the enclosed spaces for too long under intense heat posed serious health hazards. Despite the intense physical strain and hazardous working conditions, Musk persevered through the assignment to earn the crucial income he needed to advance his education and establish his independence in North America.

From Physical Hardship to Higher Education and Global Success

The intense hardship and physical exhaustion of these early jobs eventually paved the way for Elon Musk to transition into higher education and pursue his broader ambitions. Having earned and saved money through his strenuous farm duties, timber cutting, and hazardous boiler room labor, Musk secured admission to Queen's University in Ontario, Canada. Enrolling at Queen's marked a dramatic shift in his life trajectory, taking him away from manual shoveling in industrial facilities and bringing him into an academic environment focused on science, business, and economics. After spending time establishing a strong academic foundation in Ontario, Musk subsequently transferred to the University of Pennsylvania in the United States, where he completed his undergraduate studies. The stark contrast between his early days as an eighteen-year-old laborer navigating unfamiliar Canadian provinces and his later status as a global pioneer highlights the extraordinary nature of his personal journey. Reflecting on his youth, the experiences of shoveling grain in Saskatchewan storage bins and scraping toxic residue from boiling industrial units in British Columbia remained defining milestones of his youth. These challenging endeavors demonstrated a willingness to undertake grueling tasks to achieve long-term goals. Today, as he oversees groundbreaking technology enterprises, the story of his early years in Canada serves as a reminder that even the world's most prominent business figures often started from modest beginnings, surviving on limited funds and undertaking high-risk physical labor long before achieving international prominence.
