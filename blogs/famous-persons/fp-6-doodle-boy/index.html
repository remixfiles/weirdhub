He Got in Trouble for Doodling in Class. Then Nike Hired Him.

By WeirdHub Team • 2 September, 2026

From School Trouble to an Art Club

Nine-year-old Joe Whale often got into trouble at his school in Shrewsbury, England. While his teachers were teaching, Joe was busy drawing funny little characters on his books, desks, and whiteboards. Most of his teachers thought he simply could not pay attention in class. However, his parents did not punish him for his drawings. They knew their son was not being bad, but was actually deeply in love with art. To help him grow, they signed him up for an after-school art club called Bloom. His art teacher quickly saw his special gift. Joe did not need to plan his drawings or use a pencil first. He could take a simple black marker and draw complex, fun scenes directly from his mind onto any empty surface.

The Big Wall That Changed His Life

Joe's art teacher began posting pictures of his black-and-white drawings on social media, and people loved them. Soon, the owners of a local restaurant named Number 4 saw his talent online. Instead of hiring a professional painter, they invited nine-year-old Joe to decorate a large eight-foot wall in their dining room. Joe loved the idea and went to work right away. He spent about twelve hours over several days drawing directly onto the blank wall. Using only his imagination, he covered the entire space with funny monsters, smiling food, and friendly creatures. Photos of the completed wall spread fast across the internet. In a matter of days, the quiet schoolboy became famous around the world as The Doodle Boy.

From Small Doodles to Global Fame

The drawings that once got Joe into trouble at school soon opened doors to incredible opportunities. Famous global brands reached out to work with him because they loved his unique style. By the time he was twelve, sportswear giant Nike signed him to help create artwork and inspire kids everywhere to be creative. He went on to work with big names like Disney and Pixar, drew pictures for popular children's books, and even received praise from the British Royal Family. Joe's journey shows how powerful support and love can be for a young child. What looked like a bad habit in a normal classroom turned out to be a world-class talent, simply because his parents chose to believe in his dream.
