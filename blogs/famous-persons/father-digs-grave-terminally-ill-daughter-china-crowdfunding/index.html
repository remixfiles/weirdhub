Father Digs Grave to Prepare Terminally Ill Daughter for Death Before Global Support Changes Everything

By WeirdHub Team • 7 November, 2026

A Heartbreaking Decision in Rural Sichuan

In June 2017, a deeply moving story from a rural village in Sichuan Province, China, captured international attention and shed light on the intense struggles faced by impoverished families dealing with severe medical conditions. Zhang Liyong, a devoted father, learned that his two-year-old daughter, Zhang Xinlei, was suffering from severe thalassemia, a inherited blood disorder that requires continuous and costly medical care to maintain life. Over the course of her medical journey, Zhang Liyong and his wife poured all of their financial resources into their child's healthcare, eventually spending their entire personal savings of approximately 140,000 Yuan. Despite their immense sacrifices, the family reached a point where their financial options were completely exhausted, while the remaining medical expenditures required for her long-term treatment were estimated to be around 500,000 Yuan. Facing the overwhelming reality that they could no longer afford the life-sustaining care their daughter needed, the heartbroken father fell into deep emotional sorrow. In an effort to prepare both himself and his young daughter for what seemed like an inevitable tragedy, Zhang Liyong dug a shallow grave in a field near their home. His intention was to help the toddler become accustomed to the concept of death so that she would not feel afraid if her condition deteriorated further. Every day, Zhang Liyong took little Zhang Xinlei to the open grave, where he lay beside her, played games, and talked with her to offer comfort in the face of limited medical options.

A Viral Video Mobilizes Worldwide Support

The poignant visual images of Zhang Liyong lying next to his sick daughter in the grave soon began circulating on social media and international news platforms, triggering an immediate and powerful emotional response worldwide. Millions of viewers were heartbroken by the sight of a father preparing his child for death due to an inability to pay for ongoing medical care. As news outlets reported on the situation in Sichuan, online crowdfunding campaigns quickly emerged to collect donations for Zhang Xinlei's treatment. People from around the globe, moved by the family's plight, began contributing money to assist with the required medical expenses. In a short period, the public fundraising initiatives successfully gathered sufficient funds to cover the estimated medical costs of around 500,000 Yuan, relieving the heavy financial pressure that had pushed the family to despair. The dramatic turn of events showcased how global connectivity and collective empathy could intervene in moments of extreme crisis. For Zhang Liyong and his family, the overwhelming response offered a renewed sense of hope during their darkest time, transforming a desperate situation into one where proper medical care could be pursued. The international support demonstrated the profound impact of community solidarity when traditional financial means are completely exhausted.

A Path Toward Healing and Hope

Alongside the financial assistance provided by donors worldwide, the family was also exploring a critical medical procedure to offer Zhang Xinlei a long-term chance of recovery. At the time when the story became widely known, Zhang Liyong's wife was pregnant with their second child. The parents hoped to use the newborn's umbilical cord blood, which contains valuable stem cells, as part of a biological treatment strategy for the older daughter's thalassemia. Medical specialists had advised that umbilical cord blood transplantation could potentially offer a therapeutic solution for the severe blood condition. The combination of public donations for medical care and the potential match from the upcoming baby's cord blood provided a tangible pathway forward for the family's medical efforts. While social media posts later added unverified details and exaggerated claims regarding post-treatment events, the verified history remains centered on a father's profound devotion and the global support that followed. The story of Zhang Liyong and Zhang Xinlei serves as a lasting reminder of the emotional hardships endured by families battling severe pediatric conditions, as well as the vital role that financial aid and medical science play in bringing hope to desperate circumstances.
