Too Good-Looking for Fashion's Biggest Night: Why the Met Gala Quietly Fired Its Most Viral Greeter

By Oppa Heen • 10 September, 2026

An Accidental Spotlight on the Red Carpet

The Met Gala is famous for dramatic couture, high-profile celebrity drama, and carefully choreographed grandeur where every single spotlight is reserved exclusively for the rich and famous. Yet behind every A-list star making their grand entrance up the iconic museum steps, there is a dedicated team of hosts and greeters tasked with ensuring the entire spectacle runs without a hitch. In May 2023, Italian model Eugenio Casnighi was simply hired to do that modest job, stationed near the red carpet to guide VIP guests through the flashing cameras and roaring crowds. Paired directly with reality TV superstar Kylie Jenner, he quietly held his post in a sharp, classic tuxedo while maintaining absolute professional restraint. However, the internet had completely different plans that evening. Within hours of the broadcast, eagle-eyed viewers across TikTok, Instagram, and X began cropping out the celebrities to zoom in on the striking greeter standing just inches behind them. Clips highlighting his chiselled features quickly garnered millions of views and generated countless comedic memes, transforming an ordinary working student into an accidental global heartthrob overnight.

The Sudden Fall from Grace Behind the Scenes

For an event strictly designed to celebrate hyper-exclusive celebrity prestige, an ordinary staff worker inadvertently stealing the show proved to be a delicate public relations dilemma. As the 2024 gala drew near, Casnighi was scheduled and fully prepared to resume his professional role on the museum steps once again, having signed strict non-disclosure agreements regarding guest interactions. But just days before the glamorous curtain rose for the new season, everything unraveled over an unexpected phone call and a series of blunt messages. Organizers and his staffing agency abruptly informed him that his services were permanently cancelled. According to Casnighi, management bluntly accused him of making the entire previous event about himself and allowing personal vanity to eclipse the high-profile guests he was hired to serve. Devastated by the sudden termination and feeling scapegoated for organic internet viral fame that he never asked for, the young model decided he no longer had any reason to stay silent. Stripped of his promised paycheck and his credentials on fashion's grandest evening, he took directly to social media to reveal the hidden truth behind his shocking dismissal.

The Unforgiving Price of Outshining the Elite

Casnighi broke his contractual silence through a series of candid TikTok confessionals, showcasing internal communications that corroborated his sudden firing simply because he had become too recognizable. The revelation immediately sparked global debate across major news outlets, raising uncomfortable questions about elite insecurity and the fragile hierarchy maintained at ultra-exclusive celebrity galas. While event organizers and Vogue leadership opted for total silence, public sympathy swung overwhelmingly in favor of the working model who was penalized merely for doing his job with striking looks. What began as a punitive dismissal ended up delivering the ultimate poetic irony, transforming Eugenio Casnighi into a bona fide mainstream personality far beyond the museum steps. High-end brands, commercial modeling agencies, and international fashion campaigns instantly reached out to book him, proving that public fascination often outweighs elite gatekeeping. The Met Gala managed to banish him from its velvet ropes, but in doing so, they gifted the dismissed greeter a level of international fame that many seated inside the venue could only dream of buying.
