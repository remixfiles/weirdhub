They Left Him To Die With No Limbs. Four Years Later, He Broke The 100-Meter World Record.

By WH Team • 30 October, 2026

The Night A Minor Flu Turned Into A Total Nightmare

In January 2018, Davide Bartolo Morana was an energetic, twenty-four-year-old Italian living in the sunny city of Murcia, Spain, with a bright future and a passion for sports. One ordinary evening, he began experiencing mild fever and fatigue, symptoms that anyone in winter would immediately write off as a standard seasonal flu. Seeking relief, he went to the local emergency clinic, where medical professionals examined him briefly, agreed it was likely a viral infection, and sent him back home to get plenty of bed rest with standard over-the-counter painkillers. However, over the course of the following twelve hours, his physical state spiraled into absolute chaos. His mild temperature quickly turned into a scorching fever, accompanied by uncontrollable shivering, intense vomiting, and an agonizing headache that felt like a sledgehammer striking his skull. When Davide caught a glimpse of himself in the mirror the next morning, terror set in as he discovered dark purple and reddish blotches creeping rapidly across his skin, looking very much like severe burns or internal bruising. Rushed back to the hospital, his vital signs collapsed in front of the emergency room doctors, who immediately recognized the sinister reality that had slipped past them earlier. Davide was suffering from severe meningococcal meningitis, an aggressive bacterial bloodstream infection that rapidly inflames the protective membranes surrounding the brain and spinal cord while poisoning the entire circulatory system. The rapid proliferation of bacteria triggered purpura fulminans and catastrophic septic shock, causing his internal organs to shut down in rapid succession. Desperate to keep his failing brain alive, doctors placed Davide into a deep medically induced coma, gently warning his family that roughly ninety-three percent of individuals who reach this level of organ shutdown never open their eyes again.

The Miracle Wake-Up And The Brutal Price Of Survival

Davide defied every grim statistic and opened his eyes after a full week trapped in an induced coma, yet the miracle of his survival came with a heartbreaking reality. While his heart and mind had won the battle against the deadly infection, his limbs had borne the brunt of the vascular destruction. The severe septic shock had caused microscopic blood clots to choke off blood flow to his extremities, starving the tissue until all four limbs suffered complete peripheral necrosis. To prevent toxic gangrene from poisoning his remaining healthy tissue and ending his life, surgeons had no choice but to carry out urgent, extensive amputations. Within days, Davide underwent grueling surgeries that removed both of his legs below the knees and both of his arms below the elbows. Waking up in a sterile hospital bed without hands or feet was an incomprehensible shock that could easily destroy any person's spirit, plunging them into permanent despair. Instead of surrendering to darkness, Davide looked down at his bandages and chose to view the ordeal not as the end of his life, but as an extraordinary second chance at living. The subsequent road to recovery was an exhausting, agonizing gauntlet that demanded months of daily physical rehabilitation. Every single human function, from balancing his torso upright without hands to taking awkward first steps on basic mechanical prosthetics, had to be learned from zero with immense sweat and tears. Even basic daily tasks like drinking a glass of water, dressing himself, or turning a door handle felt like climbing mountains. Yet, instead of just aiming for basic survival, Davide wanted to push the human body to its absolute limits, refusing to settle for a quiet, sedentary life behind closed doors.

Running On Carbon Fiber Blades Into The History Books

Driven by an unstoppable fire inside his chest, Davide made a bold decision that astonished everyone around him: he set his sights on competitive para-athletics. He secured lightweight carbon fiber running blades and began an intense training regimen under professional track coaches, enduring blisters, muscular pain, and exhausting daily workouts. Moving on curved blades requires precise core balance and immense cardiovascular power, challenges amplified significantly when an athlete lacks biological arms to generate natural forward momentum and stabilize their center of gravity. Davide spent four unbroken years turning his sweat and discipline into blazing speed, eventually earning a coveted spot on the Italian national para-athletics team in the T62 category. The culmination of this miraculous journey arrived in July 2022 at the Italian National Paralympic Championships in Padua. Lining up in the starting blocks beside elite competitors, the young man who four years earlier had been given a seven percent chance of living exploded across the track, crossing the finish line in an astonishing twelve point thirteen seconds. It was a blazing performance that stunned global athletic circles and instantly positioned him among the fastest quad-amputee sprinters in track history. Davide proved to the entire world that sheer mental fortitude can overcome even the most catastrophic physical blows. Beyond the track, he channeled his story into his memoir, Arriba la Vida, and became a global ambassador for the Meningitis Research Foundation, spearheading the universal Meningitis Flag campaign to help families recognize the subtle early symptoms before it is too late. His life stands as undeniable proof that the human spirit cannot be broken by tragedy, transforming four lost limbs into wings of pure inspiration for millions across the planet.
