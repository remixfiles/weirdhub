Taiwanese Student Amputates Both Legs in Dry Ice for $1.3 Million Insurance Fraud Scheme

By WeirdHub Team • 9 November, 2026

A Horrifying Scheme to Claim Million-Dollar Insurance Payouts

In January 2023, a 23-year-old Taiwanese university student named Zhang embarked on an extraordinary and tragic insurance fraud scheme alongside his former schoolmate, Liao. Liao persuaded Zhang that they were trapped in severe financial debt and needed an urgent way out. Together, they devised a desperate strategy to target multiple insurance companies by fabricating a catastrophic injury to claim massive payouts. The conspirators set their sights on securing approximately 41.8 million Taiwan dollars, which equaled around 1.3 million US dollars in insurance claims. To make the injury appear genuine and unavoidable, Liao persuaded Zhang to undergo a horrifying physical ordeal that would permanently alter his life. On a fateful night, Liao tied Zhang to a chair inside a room, preparing a container filled to the brim with dry ice. For roughly ten agonizing hours, Zhang kept both of his feet completely submerged in the sub-zero substance while Liao monitored the process, capturing photographic and video evidence of the entire event. The extreme prolonged exposure to dry ice caused devastating tissue damage, leaving Zhang with severe frostbite across both lower extremities. When Zhang finally sought medical attention at a hospital, the physical damage was so catastrophic that medical professionals had no choice but to amputate both of his legs below the knees. The dramatic loss of his limbs was meant to be the cornerstone of their multi-million-dollar insurance claim, but the brutal reality of their actions quickly began to catch up with them. The pair hoped that this drastic self-inflicted harm would guarantee a massive financial settlement from the policies they had arranged, believing that the horrific nature of the injury would shield their plan from scrutiny. However, the decision to sacrifice his health for money would soon trigger a chain of events that exposed the entire plot to law enforcement authorities.

Medical Inconsistencies and Climate Data Unravel the Cover-Up

Following the life-altering amputations, Zhang and Liao filed paperwork across multiple insurance providers, claiming that the extreme frostbite occurred naturally during a late-night motorcycle ride. They asserted that the freezing nighttime weather conditions while riding on the open road had accidentally frozen Zhang's feet to the point of irreparable tissue destruction. However, insurance investigators and medical experts quickly raised serious red flags regarding the legitimacy of the claims. Medical staff observed that the pattern of Zhang's injuries did not match typical environmental frostbite, as the damage was unnaturally symmetrical and neatly localized below the knees without any corresponding signs of cold damage elsewhere on his body, such as his hands or upper legs. Furthermore, meteorological records collected from the night of the alleged motorcycle journey completely contradicted their story, showing that local temperatures were nowhere near cold enough to induce severe frostbite, let alone prompt full-limb amputations. Suspicious of the flagrant discrepancies between the environmental climate data, medical reports, and the timing of newly purchased policy agreements, the insurance companies flagged the case for potential fraud. Taiwan's Criminal Investigation Bureau launched a comprehensive investigation into the incident, eventually discovering the disturbing digital evidence, including the images and recordings of Zhang tied to the chair with his feet submerged in dry ice. The investigators meticulously pieced together the evidence, proving that the alleged accident was actually a carefully staged event executed in secret. The overwhelming scientific and physical evidence rendered their motorcycle story completely unbelievable, stripping away any defense the two former schoolmates tried to present to the investigators.

Justice Served as the Elaborate Plot Ends in Convictions

The ultimate outcome of the elaborate fraud proved disastrous for both men, yielding almost no financial reward while resulting in permanent physical disability and criminal convictions. Out of the massive 41.8 million Taiwan dollars in claims submitted to multiple insurance companies, the duo managed to collect from only a single provider, which disbursed a relatively small sum of 236,427 Taiwan dollars. Even this modest payment was swiftly confiscated by authorities once Taiwan's Criminal Investigation Bureau formally uncovered the plot and presented undeniable proof of self-inflicted injury and conspiracy. Both men were subsequently arrested and brought before the justice system to face charges related to severe fraud and conspiracy. In 2024, the court handed down its verdict, sentencing Zhang to a two-year prison sentence, which was suspended for two years in light of his circumstances and severe permanent disability. Liao, identified as the primary orchestrator who pushed the plan forward, convinced Zhang of the debt crisis, and recorded the act, received a much harsher six-year prison sentence. Liao attempted to contest the conviction, but the judicial authorities held firm, and his six-year sentence was officially upheld on appeal in 2025. The case stands as one of the most extreme recorded instances of insurance fraud, highlighting how financial desperation and manipulative influence led to irreversible self-destruction. In the end, Zhang was left without his legs, without the million-dollar fortune he had hoped to secure, and with a criminal record that marked the definitive collapse of their fraudulent scheme.
