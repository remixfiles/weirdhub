The Night a Drunk Man Took a 40-Ton Soviet Battle Tank on a Midnight Joyride

By Michael David • 28 September, 2026

A Roaring Beast in the Midnight Silence

On the warm late evening of June 12, 2019, the quiet residential neighborhood of Pajeczno, a peaceful town in central Poland, shattered under a violent, metallic roar that sounded straight out of a wartime nightmare. Around ten at night, windows rattled and the ground shook violently beneath the feet of stunned locals who peeked out through their curtains, only to see the monstrous silhouette of a Cold War era Soviet T-55 battle tank rumbling straight down Mickiewicza Street. Weighing around forty tons and belching thick diesel smoke into the night air, the tracked war machine tore across the asphalt as startled residents flooded emergency switchboards with frantic phone calls. Many thought an actual military invasion was taking place or that armed conflict had mysteriously landed on their doorstep, while others simply could not believe their eyes. What seemed like a chaotic war zone movie scene turned surreal when police cruisers sped toward the commotion, only to discover the massive steel behemoth parked dead center in the middle of a main public roadway with its massive main cannon quietly pointing forward, leaving an entire town completely paralyzed with disbelief.

An Unbelievable Joyride Fueled by Alcohol

When local police officers cautiously approached the parked monster under the streetlights, they found no rogue army or masked insurgents, but rather two very ordinary men standing casually beside the roaring iron tracks. Behind the wheel had been a local forty-nine-year-old man accompanied by a passenger he had invited along for what was perhaps the most absurd joyride in modern history. The tank had originally been scheduled for transport on a specialized flatbed trailer, but when that transport vehicle broke down, the tank was left unattended while repairs were underway. The man, whose sole official duty was to simply drive the vehicle back onto the trailer once fixing was done, decided to take matters into his own hands after consuming a reckless amount of alcohol. A subsequent breathalyzer test showed his blood alcohol content was hovering near two per mille, far above the legal driving threshold. Armed with impaired judgment and zero fear, he simply fired up the historic diesel engine and rolled the forty-ton war machine through town, completely devoid of proper heavy vehicle licensing, road permits, or vehicle insurance.

The Former Soldier and a Costly Morning Awakening

The bizarre midnight spectacle soon left law enforcement facing a unique logistical nightmare, because Polish police departments obviously do not have standard tow trucks capable of hauling a Soviet battle tank off public roads. While the heavily intoxicated driver was promptly handcuffed and thrown into a police holding cell to sober up, the tank remained stuck in the middle of town for hours under strict police guard. Desperate for a solution before morning traffic began, local authorities were forced to track down an experienced former military serviceman who possessed the rare, specialized skills required to operate vintage armored armor. At around five o'clock the following morning, just as the sun was beginning to rise, the veteran arrived, climbed inside the hatch, revived the massive engine, and steered the historic beast away to a secure impound lot. When the driver finally woke up with a pounding hangover and regained full consciousness, he was hit with devastating legal reality instead of applause. Facing two separate criminal charges, one for driving while heavily intoxicated and another far more severe charge for creating a direct catastrophe hazard to public safety, the midnight tank commander was handed up to eight years in prison, turning a wild drunken urge into one of Europe's most unbelievable and expensive lessons in history.
