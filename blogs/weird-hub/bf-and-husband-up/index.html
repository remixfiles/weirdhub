Day With The Lover, Night With The Husband: The Unbelievable Family Pact That Baffled Police

By Bombay Reporter • 7 September, 2026

A Strange Domestic Triangle Takes Root

Police officers in Mahmudabad, a quiet town in the Sitapur district of Uttar Pradesh, India, thought they had seen every possible kind of domestic conflict. That changed when a local woman named Anju walked into the police station with her husband, Shubham Verma, her lover, Nitin Vishwakarma, and her two young children. Rather than filing a complaint of infidelity or abandonment, the trio came seeking formal validation for a domestic arrangement that left officers stunned. Nitin had long been a close household acquaintance, someone Shubham regarded almost as a brother. Over time, that dynamic shifted into romance between Anju and Nitin. When the affair came to light, it did not trigger the expected divorce or hostility. Instead, the three adults had reached a quiet, unanimous consensus to share their lives together under one roof, keeping the family intact while allowing the new connection to thrive.

The Schedule That Sparked A Household War

Peace inside their home, however, did not survive the scrutiny of the extended family. While Shubham accepted the unconventional union with complete calm, his parents and relatives vehemently opposed the idea of welcoming a lover into the family home. The mounting pressure and daily disputes eventually forced the trio to take their dilemma to law enforcement. During police questioning, they laid out a remarkably methodical coexistence plan designed to prevent jealousy and domestic friction. Under this proposed schedule, Nitin would spend the daytime hours caring for the home, assisting Anju, and helping watch over the two children. As night fell, Shubham would return to resume his place as husband. To the astonishment of investigators, Shubham openly endorsed the schedule, emphasizing that he cherished both his wife and Nitin and refused to abandon either of them, even if it meant walking away from his ancestral home to rent a separate house.

The Impasse That Left The Law Helpless

Inside the police station, experienced officers spent hours conducting counseling sessions, trying to persuade the adults to reflect on the long-term emotional and social consequences of their decision. Their efforts produced no breakthrough. Anju stood firm on her conviction that she loved both men equally and would not surrender either bond. Shubham remained equally determined, choosing unconditional loyalty to his chosen partners over societal norms and family inheritance. Because all three individuals were consenting adults and no formal criminal complaints of abuse, harassment, or coercion were filed, the police ultimately had no legal authority to intervene or detain them. The trio walked out of the station together, entirely united in their resolve, leaving law enforcement bewildered and igniting a fierce public debate across India about fidelity, unconventional modern bonds, and how far people will go to redefine traditional marriage.
