Concrete Mixer Truck Parked for 45 Days in Turkey to Save a Dove Nest with Eggs

By WeirdHub Team • 9 November, 2026

A Surprising Discovery Atop Heavy Machinery in Hatay

In the busy atmosphere of an industrial concrete manufacturing plant located in the Kumluca district of Hatay province, Turkey, routine construction operations took an unexpected turn that would touch hearts far beyond the job site. The plant relies heavily on massive concrete mixer trucks that travel daily to various construction projects, maintaining a rigorous schedule of heavy labor and industrial movement. However, driver Mulla Biçer stepped up to perform his standard pre-shift safety inspection on his assigned concrete mixer truck, expecting nothing more than the typical mechanical check before embarking on a hard day of work. As he examined the upper structure of the heavy vehicle, he noticed something completely out of the ordinary perched atop the large rotating mixer barrel. A pair of wild doves had selected the metal surface of the heavy machinery as the foundation for a tiny nest, using bits of twigs and organic material to create a humble shelter directly on top of the construction equipment. Upon closer inspection, Mulla Biçer realized that the birds had not merely built a temporary sanctuary; they had already laid eggs inside the carefully constructed nest. Recognizing the extreme vulnerability of the fragile eggs atop a heavy machine designed for harsh industrial labor, the experienced driver immediately halted his routine operations. Turning on the engine or operating the rotating mixer would have instantly destroyed the nest and crushed the eggs inside. Rather than disregarding the small creatures or clearing the obstruction to maintain his work schedule, Mulla Biçer immediately reported his discovery to the company management. He detailed the exact location of the nest and highlighted the grave danger the birds would face if the concrete mixer were put into operation. The management team listened carefully to the report and examined the situation firsthand, recognizing that immediate human intervention was necessary to protect the avian family. This initial discovery set the stage for an unprecedented response within an industrial setting, demonstrating how brief moments of empathy from observant workers can alter the course of everyday commercial operations and spark a remarkable effort to safeguard innocent lives.

Halting Machinery and Daily Care by Dedicated Workers

Following the discovery reported by driver Mulla Biçer, the management of the concrete manufacturing firm in Hatay province joined hands with their workers to make a compassionate decision that prioritized animal life over immediate financial productivity. Company officials and Mulla Biçer collectively agreed that the concrete mixer truck would be completely removed from active service and kept turned off until the eggs hatched, the newborn chicks grew strong enough, and the young birds could fly away safely on their own. Knowing that multiple workers operated within the busy industrial facility and that someone might accidentally attempt to start the heavy machinery without realizing a delicate nest was resting on top, the team took immediate preventive measures. They placed a clearly visible physical warning sign directly near the nest, bearing the explicit message Caution, there is a bird nest here to alert all site personnel and drivers to keep the engine completely silent. With the truck parked safely and key operations suspended, the commitment to protect the avian family extended far beyond simply turning off the engine. Employees at the facility actively took on the responsibility of monitoring and caring for the birds throughout the incubation period. Every morning, company employee Hamza Cankir, along with other dedicated members of the staff, made it a point to visit the parked concrete mixer truck. Hamza Cankir ensured that fresh food and clean water were provided regularly near the nesting area, creating a safe and supportive environment for the mother dove as she sat on her eggs. The site staff maintained this peaceful atmosphere day after day, keeping close watch over the vessel while ensuring that the mother bird remained undisturbed by routine industrial noise. As the incubation period progressed, the eggs finally hatched, revealing fragile newborn chicks that required constant feeding, protection, and patient oversight from both their parents and the watchful company staff. This cooperative effort highlighted an extraordinary level of dedication from everyone involved, transforming a standard construction facility into a sanctuary of human kindness and active animal welfare.

Successful Flight into the Sky and Return to Industrial Work

The quiet sanctuary established atop the stationary concrete mixer continued without interruption as the days turned into weeks. Day after day, company staff maintained their daily routine of providing sustenance and monitoring the progress of the growing chicks, ensuring that no industrial activity disturbed the resting birds. A total of forty-five consecutive days passed while the concrete mixer truck remained parked and silent at the manufacturing site in the Kumluca district of Hatay province. Throughout this entire period, employee Hamza Cankir and his fellow workers diligently provided food and water, allowing the mother dove to raise her young in absolute safety. As the weeks went by, the tiny hatchlings grew stronger, developing their feathers and building the muscular strength required for their very first flight. The patience and financial sacrifice of the concrete company culminated in late September 2024, when driver Mulla Biçer and the facility staff witnessed the ultimate reward for their long wait. The young birds, having reached full maturity and readiness, spread their wings and safely took flight into the sky, permanently departing the metal nest that had sheltered them since their unhatched beginnings. Mulla Biçer and his colleagues watched with immense satisfaction as the birds soared away, knowing their careful vigilance and forty-five days of suspended vehicle operation had successfully preserved the lives of the entire avian family. Only after confirming that the nest was completely vacated and the birds had safely flown into the wild did the company return the heavy concrete mixer truck to active duty. Media reports published in Turkey near the end of September 2024 brought this story to international attention, detailing how Mulla Biçer, Hamza Cankir, and the concrete firm chose kindness over commercial convenience. The decision to park a heavy industrial vehicle for forty-five full days stands as a powerful testament to environmental stewardship and human empathy within modern industry, proving that human compassion can flourish even in the most unexpected industrial environments.
