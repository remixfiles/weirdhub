The Giant Bird That Flew Across Town Every Morning Just to Attend Kindergarten

By Mariyam Shignal • 5 September, 2026

An Unbelievable Morning Visitor at the Schoolyard

Every morning in the quiet coastal city of Ube, Japan, children at a local kindergarten gathered outside, gazing at the horizon waiting for a very unusual classmate. Right on schedule, a massive white silhouette with an impressive wingspan would appear over the rooftops, descending gracefully onto the courtyard. It was not a stray pet or a local stray dog, but a giant great white pelican named Katta-kun. Born in 1985 at nearby Tokiwa Park, the majestic bird had been hand-raised by caretakers, making him remarkably comfortable around human beings. In the summer of 1989, however, he decided the boundaries of the park were not enough. He took to the skies on his own accord, circled over the neighborhood, and touched down right in the middle of the schoolyard, instantly leaving both teachers and toddlers completely awestruck.

A Heartwarming Bond That Defied the Laws of Nature

What could have easily turned into a moment of panic quickly blossomed into one of the purest friendships ever documented. Instead of showing fear or territorial aggression, Katta-kun strolled calmly up to the windows to peer inside the classrooms, gently tapping the glass while lessons were underway. The children welcomed the gentle giant with open arms, petting his smooth feathers and resting their heads against his wings during recess. Teachers and local residents happily obliged his massive appetite, feeding him fresh fish as he waddled playfully beside the kids. When afternoon came and the school day drew to a close, Katta-kun would simply spread his wings, take off into the evening breeze, and glide back across the city to his home at the park lake.

The Lasting Legacy of Japan's Most Beloved Classmate

As national television networks broadcast footage of this extraordinary bond, Katta-kun became an instant sensation throughout Japan, even inspiring an animated feature film and becoming the official symbol of the city. For nearly two decades, he remained an iconic presence, proving to millions worldwide that pure trust can exist between wild creatures and humankind. The devoted pelican lived a long, cherished life before passing away peacefully of old age in 2008 at the age of twenty-three. Today, a permanent memorial stands in Ube, preserving the unforgettable memory of the bird who simply wanted to go to kindergarten every single day.
